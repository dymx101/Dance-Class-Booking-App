Automated Studio Reports

Delivering studio performance insights directly to your inbox or chat.

1. Report Types

  • Weekly Performance Recap Revenue totals, most popular classes, and fill rates for the past 7 days.
  • Monthly Growth Deep-Dive Retention cohorts, member sign-ups, and long-term financial trends.
  • Daily Attendance Alert Summary of today's bookings, cancellations, and no-shows.

2. Delivery Channels

Email (PDF Summary)

Professional formatted email with charts and data tables.

WeChat Official Account

Instant push notification with key numbers to the studio owner's WeChat.

Technical Engine

We will use Supabase Cron (pg_net) to trigger a weekly Edge Function. This function will query our existing analytics views, generate a summary, and deliver it via an email service (Resend) or WeChat API.
