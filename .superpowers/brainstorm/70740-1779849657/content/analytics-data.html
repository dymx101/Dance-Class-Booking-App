Design Section 1: Analytics Data Strategy

Server-side aggregation for high-performance dashboards.

Efficient Data Views

view_revenue_stats

Aggregates purchase_records by week/month. Calculates total CNY and card counts.

view_teacher_performance

Joins teachers with bookings. Calculates average fill rate and waitlist length per instructor.

view_member_growth

Analyzes users signup dates and last booking activity to detect active vs. churned members.

Aggregation Engine

  • Atomic Calculations Instead of fetching thousands of bookings to the browser, the Postgres View does the math. The frontend only receives the final numbers (e.g. "94%").
  • Secure Access Analytics views are strictly protected by the is_admin() policy. Normal members have zero access to these data aggregations.

Benefit

"Your dashboard stays lightning fast even with 10,000 members and 50,000 bookings."
